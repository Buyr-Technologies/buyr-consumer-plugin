# Get started with Buyr

Buyr lets you name your price on products from the Buyr store. You find a product, make an offer, and complete checkout on the store when your offer is accepted.

## What you can do

1. Find a negotiable product on the Buyr store.
2. Name your price and preview the quote before you submit.
3. Track the offer after you submit.
4. Check out on the store when the offer is accepted.

Anonymous search and quotes work without signing in. Submit, history, and checkout require a linked shopper account.

## Example prompts

- Find negotiable running shoes under $120.
- What is the best price I can offer on this product?
- Show my open Buyr offers.
